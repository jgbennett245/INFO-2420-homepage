Website Critiques
  1. My first impression of the utah.com/destinations/national-parks site was good, the site is well organized and easy to read. The one exception is the promotional links section didn't work, but that is because of my firewall, rather than the website itself.
  2. The site can be navigated through a series of links on the left side, which take the user to specific parts of the page. The Explore this page box on the left side of the page contains links to information like maps, FAQs and videos etc. Below this is another heading called Utah National Parks which lists all five of Utah's national parks and provides info about them. Below this is the Trip Planning heading which contains things like tour guides.
  3. The structure of the site does make sense and isn't confusing, we get enough images and information about each park without it being overwhelming.
  4. The media enhances the site, it is laid out logically and helps the user understand the information on the site. While showing some of the highlights of each National Park and the reasons for going there.
  5. The load time was fast, the site seems to have no major issues that would impact performance.
  6. The background is white at the header of the page and light grey in the page body. The text is standard black with blue for links. The text is easy to read and the background doesn't detract from this.
  7. I have already mentioned navigation in my overall impressions section, so I won't go into exhaustive detail here. A search box is present at the top of the page, though this is for more general navigation of the site itself, as opposed to this page exclusively.
  8. The color scheme was consistent across all pages.
  9. The site works fine at smaller resolutions and screen sizes. I tested at 1920x1080 and 960x540 and it seems to be fine.
  10. This site does nothing avant-garde or spectacular, it is just a well made utilitarian website.
